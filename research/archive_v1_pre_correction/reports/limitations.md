# Limitations

1. **TASC-v0 was tuned on Landsat D2 only** (tau = 0.55, etc.). It was not re-tuned for any other dataset (no methodology change was permitted); several negative results below follow from that.
2. **D2 circularity.** D2 shadows are synthesised from the same DEM that DEM-assisted TASC uses, with the true generating sun angles. DEM benefits on D2 are an upper bound on what would happen with independent shadows.
3. **No real ground truth for illumination.** In Sentinel-2 the terrain-shadow masks are derived from the Copernicus DEM, the same source that drives TASC-DEM; metrics that use them favour DEM-aware methods. Sentinel-2 SCL was not used as ground truth. Material classes come from spectral indices (proxies). Atacama has almost no vegetation/water; winter Alpine/Rocky scenes have little vegetation, so the vegetation-based equalisation metric rests on few pixels.
4. **Few independent regions.** Sentinel-2 has 4 development regions and 2 held-out regions. With n = 4 (or 2) a Wilcoxon test cannot reach conventional significance, so those comparisons carry bootstrap intervals only and are descriptive. Held-out results are not used for any tuning.
5. **Synthetic low light.** AID and iSAID-dark low-light images are synthetic degradations; full-reference scores measure agreement with the bright original, which penalises over-brightening. DarkRS and ExDark are real low-light images but have no reference, so only no-reference metrics (BRISQUE, NIQE, entropy, EME, gradient) apply; these metrics are known to be imperfect proxies for perceived quality.
6. **Unit independence.** iSAID-dark has 138 units of which 72 are three darkness levels of 24 high-pixel scenes. D2 units are tiles from a limited number of Landsat scenes. Intervals for these are probably too narrow.
7. **Baselines.** Not evaluated: SAGC and SAMSIE (no verified official code), DFFN (downloaded, not executed, see `dataset_audit.md`). "2D-gamma" is an independent re-implementation in the style of Lee et al. and must not be read as equivalent to any published SAGC/SAMSIE result. AGC, AGCWD, HE, CLAHE, GlobalGamma are re-implementations (provenance in `trs/methods.py` PROVENANCE). Zero-DCE uses the official Epoch99 weights through a PyTorch re-implementation verified against the v0 NumPy port (max difference 3e-7). C-correction and SCS+C are standard topographic corrections applied to the 4-band data.
8. **Downstream task.** Only an AID scene-classification linear probe (frozen ImageNet ResNet-50 features + logistic regression trained on 900 clean disjoint AID images, evaluated on the untouched 210 test images). Detection and segmentation were not run. The 'Clean-reference' accuracy (0.881) is the probe on undegraded test images.
9. **Efficiency measurements.** Per-image times inside the experiment stores were collected with 4 worker processes sharing the machine and are not comparable across stages. `efficiency.csv` (single process, 1 MP, 4 CPU threads, no GPU) gives the only comparable runtimes. The peak-RSS column of `efficiency.csv` is identical for all methods (4397 MB) because the measurement is dominated by the shared parent/interpreter footprint; **peak memory per method is therefore not reported**. No GPU was used (CPU-only PyTorch).
10. **Metric sign conventions.** `dark_to_shadow_lift` is lift(dark but lit)/lift(shadow) (lower = more selective to shadow); `equalisation_gain` is the reduction of |log ratio| of vegetation brightness in shadow vs. lit (higher = better). Both are defined in `trs/metrics2.py` and were fixed before the held-out evaluation.
11. **PSNR is not geometric accuracy.** On D2 an incorrect, lower sun elevation (-7 degrees) gave higher PSNR than the true metadata because a larger shadow field lifts under-exposed images more.
12. One Sentinel-2 field (`scl_valid_frac`) from the first fetch is wrong and unused; validity masks are derived from SCL at load time.
13. Licences: ExDark, DarkRS and iSAID images are not redistributed in this repository; only aggregated tables and Sentinel-2-based figures are committed.
